## Binaural beats

There are no binaural beat recordings here on purpose. A binaural beat is two
pure sine tones, one per ear, a few hertz apart. Mushin generates that pair
live, so the difference is exact for every session, loops forever with no seam,
and cannot be damaged by compression. A recording would be fixed at whatever
frequency it happened to be made at.
